DAY 01 – 24 APRIL 2027 (SATURDAY) GUWAHATI TO TEZPUR - PICKUP
& TRANSFER (APPROX 3-4 HOURS)
Meet our representative upon your arrival at Guwahati Airport, where you’ll be
warmly welcomed. Begin your scenic drive to Tezpur, a charming town often
referred to as the “City of Eternal Romance,” nestled along the banks of the
mighty Brahmaputra River. Surrounded by lush greenery and cultural heritage,
Tezpur offers a perfect introduction to the natural beauty of Assam.
Upon arrival, check into your hotel and relax after the journey. Spend the evening
at leisure, enjoying the calm surroundings of this peaceful town.
Overnight stay at Tezpur.

HALFDAY SIGHTSEEING (APPROX 3-4 HOURS)
After breakfast, begin your picturesque drive towards Bomdila, a beautiful hill
town located at an altitude of about 8,200 ft in Arunachal Pradesh. As the road

DAY 03 – 26 APRIL 2027(MONDAY) BOMDILA TO TAWANG - TRANSFER
VIA SELA PASS (APPROX 4-5 HOURS)
After breakfast, embark on a breathtaking journey to Tawang, one of Arunachal
Pradesh’s most spectacular destinations. En route, pass through the majestic Sela
Pass (13,700 ft.), a high-altitude mountain pass adorned with colorful prayer flags
and surrounded by snow-capped peaks.
Stop at the serene Sela Lake, a crystal-clear lake that reflects the beauty of the
surrounding mountains. Continue your drive while visiting the historic Jaswant
Garh War Memorial, built in honor of the brave soldier Rifleman Jaswant Singh
Rawat. You will also witness the magnificent Nuranang (Bong Bong) Waterfall,
one of the most beautiful waterfalls in the region.
Upon arrival in Tawang, check into your hotel and relax after the scenic journey.
Overnight stay at Tawang.
STAY: - GADHEN HOTEL / SIMILAR
MEAL: -BREAKFAST & DINNER

DAY 04 – 27 APRIL 2027 (TUESDAY) TAWANG - HIGH ALTITUDE LAKE &
BUMLA PASS
After breakfast, visit the serene Shonga-tser (Madhuri) Lake and the picturesque

beautiful PTSO Lake for photos. Return to Tawang in the afternoon and unwind,
soaking in the peaceful mountain surroundings. Overnight stay in Tawang.

MEAL: -BREAKFAST & DINNER
DAY 05 – 28 APRIL 2027(WEDNESDAY) TAWANG TO DIRANG -
TRANSFER & HALFDAY SIGHTSEEING (APPROX 4-5 HOURS)
After breakfast, begin your scenic drive to Dirang, a picturesque Himalayan
valley known for its pleasant climate and beautiful landscapes.
Upon arrival, check into your hotel and relax. Later, explore the charming
attractions of Dirang including the historic Dirang Dzong, an ancient tribal fort
offering panoramic valley views.
Spend the evening enjoying the tranquil atmosphere of this serene mountain
town.
Overnight stay at Dirang.
