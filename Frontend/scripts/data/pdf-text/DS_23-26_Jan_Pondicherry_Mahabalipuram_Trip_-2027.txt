DETAILED ITINERARY
DAY 01: 23 JAN (SATURDAY) ARRIVAL IN CHENNAI – TRANSFER TO
PONDICHERRY
Start your flight from your state to Chennai. After landing in Chennai and meet
your tour buddy, start your beautiful road trip to Pondicherry and Check into
hotel. After rest we will proceed for Pondicherry Sightseeing and Dinner
Pondicherry a former French colony is one of the best places to get a glimpse of
colonial heritage with its lanes having small yellow walled houses along with
French cafes serving delicious steaks.
The older part of Pondicherry is a stark contrast to the surrounding South Indian
cities with small houses, hotels, cafes, street-side shops selling an alternate world
of French to the Indians. We will go through the French settlements, and have
exotic French cuisine for lunch and then Visit Sri Aurobindo Ashram, Pondicherry
Museum, Sri Manakula Vinayagar Temple, and French War Memorial, The Basilica
of the Sacred Heart, Immaculate Conception Cathedral and Promenade beach. In
the evening check in to hotel for overnight stay and sumptuous dinner at the hotel
itself.
