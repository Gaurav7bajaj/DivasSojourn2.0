DAY 2: 27 JUNE (SUNDAY) FULL DAY SAFARI EXPERIENCE IN MASAI
MARA
Start your day early with a morning game drive inside the vast plains of Masai
Mara. Early mornings are one of the best times to witness wildlife activity as
animals emerge from their nighttime shelters. During the safari, you may spot
lions, elephants, cheetahs, leopards, zebras, giraffes, wildebeests, buffaloes, and
various bird species.
Enjoy a unique Bush Breakfast experience in the wilderness surrounded by scenic
landscapes. Continue exploring the reserve with additional game viewing
opportunities and enjoy a picnic lunch amidst nature. Later in the evening,
proceed for another exciting game drive to witness the beauty of the African
sunset and wildlife activity before returning to camp for dinner.
Optional: Hot Air Balloon Safari (additional cost)
STAY: - LA MAISON ROYALE MARA CAMP / SIMILAR
MEAL: - BREAKFAST, PICNIC LUNCH & DINNER

DAY 4: 29 JUNE (TUESDAY) MAASAI MARA – NAIVASHA
After breakfast, check out from the camp and drive towards Naivasha, a beautiful
destination located in Kenya’s Great Rift Valley region. Enjoy scenic landscapes
during the journey before arriving at your resort and checking in.
Later, visit Crescent Island for a walking safari experience where you can walk
among giraffes, zebras, antelopes, and other herbivores in a peaceful natural
environment. The area is also known for birdlife and stunning lake views, making
it a relaxing contrast to the safari experience in Masai Mara.

DAY 5: 30 JUNE (WEDNESDAY) EXCURSION TO LAKE NAKURU
NATIONAL PARK
After breakfast, proceed towards Lake Nakuru National Park for a full-day safari
excursion. The park is well known for its rhino population, flamingos, scenic lake
views, and rich wildlife diversity.
Enjoy game drives through the park while spotting animals such as rhinos,
giraffes, buffaloes, zebras, antelopes, and occasionally lions. The park’s
landscapes, acacia forests, and birdlife make it one of Kenya’s most picturesque
safari destinations. After the excursion, return to Naivasha for dinner and
overnight stay.
