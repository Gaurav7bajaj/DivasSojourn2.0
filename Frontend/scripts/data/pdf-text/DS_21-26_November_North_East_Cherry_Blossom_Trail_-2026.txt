DAY 3: 23 NOV (MONDAY) SHILLONG - LOCAL SIGHTSEEING
After breakfast, we will head to Don Bosco Museum of Shillong, a realm of North-
East culture and traditional knowledge. Explore an expansive collection of motifs,
artifacts, and representations of the myriad tribes of the region. Next, we will
proceed to Elephant Falls, named after a rock resembling an elephant which was
destroyed in the earthquake of 1897. The breathtaking Elephant Falls are referred
to as ‘Ka Kshaid Lai Pateng Khohsiew’ by the local Khasi people, which means ‘The
Three Step Waterfalls’, as these falls consist of three mesmerizing falls in
succession. Later, we will visit the Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians Church.
The church was built over 50 years ago and, with its high arches and stained-glass
windows, is said to be one of the most beautiful buildings in the city of Shillong.
A distinguishing fact about the church is that it is constructed to resist
earthquakes. We will then visit the beautiful Ward’s Lake, a charming man-made
lake surrounded by lush gardens, where you can enjoy a peaceful stroll and
admire the scenic surroundings. Conclude the day by taking a stroll at Police
Bazaar and indulging in some local shopping. Overnight stay at Shillong
HOTEL: HOTEL HEIGAVNS / SIMILAR
MEAL: BREAKFAST AND DINNER
