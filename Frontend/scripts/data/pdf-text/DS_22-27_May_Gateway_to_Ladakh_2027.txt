STAY: - KARMA INN/SIMILAR

DAY 4: 25 MAY (TUESDAY) NUBRA TO PANGONG LAKE
After early Breakfast visit Diskit Monestary, early morning and move for Pangong
Lake (14,500 ft), Arrival at Pangong Lake it is the highest salt water Lake in the
World, shared by two countries India & China. Enjoy the beauty of the lake on the
Banks of Pangong Lake with multi-color Reflections and fascinating high altitude
of the Lake. Dinner and overnight Night at the Cottage.
STAY: - LAKE TSO HOUSE / SIMILAR
MEAL: - BREAKFAST AND DINNER
