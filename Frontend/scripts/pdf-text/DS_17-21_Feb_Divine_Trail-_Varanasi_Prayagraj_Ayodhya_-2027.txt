DETAILED ITINERARY
DAY 01 | 17 FEB (WEDNESDAY) ARRIVAL IN VARANASI & LOCAL
SIGHTSEEING
Arrive at Varanasi Airport and proceed to the hotel for check-in and freshening
up. Later, visit the Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, followed by the Kaal
Bhairav Temple. Continue to Banaras Hindu University (BHU) Campus and Durga
Temple. Later, visit Manikarnika Ghat, one of the most sacred ghats of Varanasi.
In the evening, proceed to Dashashwamedh Ghat to witness the mesmerizing
Ganga Aarti, a deeply spiritual and captivating experience. Return to the hotel for
an overnight stay in Varanasi.
HOTEL: - ARCADIA HOTEL VARANASI / SIMILAR
MEAL: - DINNER
DAY 02 | 18 FEB (THURSDAY) VARANASI & SARNATH SIGHTSEEING
Early morning, visit the revered Kashi Vishwanath Temple, one of the twelve
Jyotirlingas dedicated to Lord Shiva. Also visit Annapurna Temple and Kashi
Vishalakshi Shakti Peeth. Return to the hotel for breakfast and check out. Later,
drive to Sarnath, approximately 10 km from Varanasi, and explore the historic
Dhamek Stupa and Chaukhandi Stupa. Return to Varanasi and check in at the hotel.

DAY 03 | 19 FEB (FRIDAY) VARANASI – PRAYAGRAJ
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and drive to Prayagraj, approximately
120 km / 3–4 hours. Upon arrival, visit the sacred Triveni Sangam, the confluence
of the Ganga, Yamuna and the mythical Saraswati. A Boat Ride to the Sangam
can be enjoyed (Direct Payment Basis), subject to availability. Later, visit Akshaya
Vat, Bade Hanuman Temple, famous for its reclining Hanuman Ji idol, Alopi
Shakti Peeth and Anand Bhawan Museum. Check in at the hotel and relax.
Overnight stay in Prayagraj.
HOTEL: - GRAND CONTINENTAL HOTEL / SIMILAR
MEAL: - BREAKFAST & DINNER
DAY 04 | 20 FEB (SATURDAY) PRAYAGRAJ – AYODHYA
After an early breakfast, check out from the hotel and drive to Ayodhya,
approximately 164 km / 3–4 hours. Upon arrival, check in at the hotel and relax.
Later, visit Hanuman Garhi, followed by Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple, one of
the most significant spiritual landmarks of Ayodhya. Continue to Dashrath Mahal
and Kanak Bhawan, dedicated to Lord Rama and Goddess Sita. Later, visit Saryu
Ghat and witness the beautiful Saryu Aarti, followed by a visit to Nageshwar Nath
Temple. Evening is free for local exploration and shopping. Overnight stay in
Ayodhya.
HOTEL: - CYGNETT COLLECTION KK HOTEL / SIMILAR
MEAL: - BREAKFAST & DINNER
