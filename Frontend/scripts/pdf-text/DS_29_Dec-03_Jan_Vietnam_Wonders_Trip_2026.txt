DETAILED ITINERARY
DAY 1 | 29 DEC 2026 | TUESDAY|ARRIVAL HANOI – HA LONG BAY
CRUISE
Arrive Hanoi airport, the driver will pick up and direct transfer to Ha Long bay,
which is about 160 km or 2.5-hour drive away. Stop half way to visit a handicraft
workshop of fine clothes and embroidered paintings. Arrive in Ha Long City at
11:30 AM and check in aboard the booked junk right after the welcome drink is
Served. Start cruising while having lunch on board and enjoy the mighty bay view.
Ha Long Bay meaning Descending Dragon Bay in Vietnamese as legend has it that
it was where a holly dragon landed down from sky. The bay cruise will continue
with up close sighting of Dinh Huong Island, Ga Choi Island, Dog Island, Sail
Island, a visit to Sung Sot Cave and swimming stop at Titov Beach. Relax to enjoy
the bay view at sunset, have dinner after, go fishing at 09:00 PM and spend a
night on board.
STAY: - AMANDA HA LONG CRUISE/SIMILAR
MEALS: LUNCH & DINNER

DIVAS SOJOURN - We Create Memories
Morning, transfer to the airport for your flight to Da Nang.
Upon arrival in Da Nang, meet your tour leader and proceed to Hoi An Ancient
Town. En route, enjoy a Basket Boat Tour at Cam Thanh Coconut Village, where
you will be warmly welcomed by the local people. Cruise through the peaceful
coconut waterways in traditional basket boats, learn the art of crab fishing, and
witness the entertaining basket boat spinning performances by the local rowers.
In the afternoon, explore the charming Hoi An Ancient Town, visiting its famous
attractions including the Japanese Covered Bridge, Pottery Museum, 200-year-
old Tan Ky Ancient House, Chua Ong Pagoda, and the bustling local market. Enjoy
a leisurely walking tour through the narrow streets lined with beautifully
preserved ancient houses and traditional assembly halls that reflect Hoi An's rich
multicultural heritage.
STAY: - SANTA LUXURY DA NANG HOTEL /SIMILAR
MEAL: - BREAKFAST & DINNER
DAY 5 | 02 JAN 2027 |SATURDAY| FULL DAY BA NA HILLS & GOLDEN
BRIDGE
After breakfast, you will be picked up from your hotel in Da Nang and transferred
to the breathtaking Ba Na Hills.
Begin your journey with a ride on one of the world's longest and most scenic cable
cars from Suoi Mo Cable Car Station to Marseille Station, offering spectacular
panoramic views of the surrounding mountains and lush forests.
Upon arrival, visit the world-famous Golden Bridge, an architectural masterpiece
supported by two giant stone hands, providing stunning views of the hills below.
Continue to Jardin D’Amour, a beautifully landscaped flower garden adorned
with colorful blooms, fountains, and artistic sculptures. Later, visit the serene Linh
Ung Pagoda, home to the magnificent statue of the Goddess of Mercy.
