DAY 02 | 18 APRIL (SUNDAY) TBILISI OLD CITY TOUR & CHRONICLES OF
GEORGIA
After breakfast, begin the Tbilisi sightseeing tour at 10:00 AM. Visit Sameba
Cathedral and explore the historic Old City, starting with Abanotubani, famous
for its sulfur baths and charming old houses. Continue towards the Juma Mosque
area and walk through the narrow streets of Old Tbilisi. Proceed to Kartlis Deda,
offering panoramic views over the city. Explore Meidan Bazaar, an atmospheric
underground market offering Georgian souvenirs, traditional crafts and local
products. Continue to Sharden Street, known for its cafés, shops and charming
lanes, and visit the iconic Gabriadze Clock Tower. Explore Rike Park and walk
across the famous Bridge of Peace. Continue to Metekhi, located on a high cliff
with beautiful panoramic views of Tbilisi. Later, visit the Chronicles of Georgia, an

DAY 05 | 21 APRIL (WEDNESDAY) TBILISI – BAGRATASHEN – HAGHPAT
– SANAHIN – DILIJAN – LAKE SEVAN – YEREVAN
After crossing the Georgia–Armenia border at Bagratashen, begin your Armenian
journey through the spectacular landscapes of Lori Province. The route takes you
through forested mountains and the dramatic Debed Gorge, offering a beautiful
introduction to Armenia. Visit Haghpat Monastery, a UNESCO World Heritage
Site and one of the finest examples of medieval Armenian architecture. Continue
to Sanahin Monastery, another remarkable medieval complex that was once an
important centre of education, theology and manuscript production. Both
monasteries preserve centuries of Armenian spiritual and intellectual heritage
through their atmospheric stone churches and courtyards.
Continue south through the mountains towards Dilijan, one of Armenia's most
picturesque resort towns. Surrounded by the forests of Dilijan National Park, the
town is known for its peaceful atmosphere, traditional architecture and artisan
heritage. In the afternoon, continue to Lake Sevan, one of Armenia's most scenic
destinations. Visit the Sevan Peninsula and Sevanavank Monastery, while
enjoying panoramic views of the beautiful lake. Continue towards Yerevan and
arrive in the late afternoon. Check in at the hotel and relax.
STAY: - ANI GRAND YEREVAN/ SIMILAR

DAY 06 | 22 APRIL (FRIDAY) YEREVAN – GEGHARD – SYMPHONY OF THE
STONES – GARNI – YEREVAN CITY TOUR
After breakfast, proceed for a full-day excursion. Visit Geghard Monastery, a
UNESCO World Heritage Site and an impressive medieval monastery partially
carved into the surrounding rock. Continue to the Symphony of the Stones, a
spectacular natural basalt formation in the Garni Gorge. Visit Garni Temple, the
only surviving pagan temple in Armenia, dating back to the 1st century AD. Enjoy
a traditional Lavash Baking Master Class followed by lunch featuring homemade
Armenian cuisine. Return to Yerevan and begin the city tour, including Abovyan
Street, Charles Aznavour Square, Opera House, Swan Lake, Arno Babajanyan
Monument, France Square and Cascade Complex, offering panoramic views of
Yerevan.
