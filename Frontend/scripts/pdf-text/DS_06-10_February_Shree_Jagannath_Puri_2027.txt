DAY 1:06 FEB (SATURDAY) ARRIVAL BHUBANESWAR – LOCAL
SIGHTSEEING
Arrive at Bhubaneswar Airport, where our cheerful Tour Leader will welcome you
and assist with the transfer to your hotel. After freshening up, embark on your
first adventure at the world-famous Nandankanan Zoological Park – a unique
combination of zoo and botanical garden. Witness exotic wildlife, enjoy the
thrilling Lion & White Tiger Safari, and soak in the lush green surroundings. Return
to the hotel for dinner.
Please Note: Only Zoo Enterance ticket is included, Different Safaries, Hiring of
Battery vehicle, Ropeway are as per actual.

DAY 3: 08 FEB (MONDAY) BHUBANESWAR – KONARK – PURI
After breakfast, check out and begin your scenic journey along the Marine Drive
towards Puri. En route, visit the serene Chandrabhaga Beach and the sacred
Ramchandi Temple. The highlight of the day is the magnificent Konark Sun Temple
(Black Pagoda) – a UNESCO World Heritage Site, adorned with intricate stone
carvings depicting celestial beauty and life.
By evening, arrive in Puri, check into your resort, and unwind at the Puri Golden
Beach, watching the waves of the Bay of Bengal.

This morning, seek divine blessings at the world-famous Jagannath Temple (Non-
Hindus can admire the temple from outside). Visit the Gundicha Temple

Later, drive to Satapada on Chilika Lake, Asia’s largest brackish water lagoon. Take
