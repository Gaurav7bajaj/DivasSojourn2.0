DETAILED ITINERARY
DAY 1- 20 MARCH (SATURDAY) ARRIVAL IN AIZAWL
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed to Dimapur Airport to
board your flight to Aizawl. Upon arrival at Aizawl Airport, meet and greet by our
representative/driver and transfer to the hotel. Check-in and relax after the
journey. The rest of the day is at leisure to explore the surroundings or unwind at
the hotel.
STAY: - HOTEL REGENCY (DELUXE) / SIMILAR
MEAL: - DINNER
DAY 2- 21 MARCH (SUNDAY) AIZAWL CITY SIGHTSEEING
After breakfast, proceed for a full-day sightseeing tour of Aizawl, the beautiful
capital city of Mizoram. Visit Solomon’s Temple, known for its impressive
architecture and peaceful surroundings. Continue to Falkawn Traditional Village,
where you can experience the traditional lifestyle, culture and heritage of the
Mizo people.
Later, visit the Mizoram State Museum, which showcases the rich history,
culture, traditions and tribal heritage of Mizoram. In the evening, enjoy the
Aizawl Skywalk and admire panoramic views of the city and surrounding hills.
STAY: - HOTEL REGENCY (DELUXE) / SIMILAR
MEAL: - BREAKFAST AND DINNER

DAY 4- 23 MARCH (TUESDAY) AGARTALA SIGHTSEEING
After breakfast, proceed for a full-day sightseeing tour of Agartala. Begin with a
visit to Maa Tripureswari Temple, one of the most revered temples of Tripura
and an important pilgrimage site.Continue to Neermahal Palace, the beautiful
Water Palace located in the middle of Rudrasagar Lake. Enjoy the scenic
surroundings and explore the palace, which reflects the royal heritage of
Tripura.Later, visit Sipahijala Wildlife Sanctuary, known for its lush greenery,
natural beauty and diverse flora and fauna.Return to Agartala after sightseeing.
Overnight stay in Agartala.

DAY 5- 24 MARCH (WEDNESDAY) AGARTALA – KUMARGHAT VIA
UNAKOTI
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed towards Kumarghat. En
route, visit Unakoti, one of Tripura’s most fascinating archaeological and religious
sites. Explore the ancient rock-cut sculptures and massive stone carvings
dedicated to Lord Shiva, surrounded by beautiful forested hills.
After exploring Unakoti, continue your journey towards Kumarghat. Upon arrival,
check in to the hotel and relax.
Overnight stay in Kumarghat.
STAY: - HOTEL ADHUNIK / SIMILAR
MEAL: - BREAKFAST AND DINNER
DAY 6- 25 MARCH (THURSDAY) KUMARGHAT – JAMPUI HILLS
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and drive towards the picturesque
Jampui Hills, one of Tripura’s most scenic hill destinations.
Enjoy the beautiful mountain roads and panoramic views of the surrounding
valleys and villages during the journey. En route, experience the local landscapes
and visit nearby local attractions, depending on time and accessibility.
Upon arrival at Jampui Hills, check in to the hotel/resort and relax amidst the
peaceful surroundings.
Overnight stay in Jampui Hills.
